Tokens

  • + circle, then a token: attach. Select first works too.
  • Box-select several: fills this entry and the next ones. Minions take them all.
  • Right-click a token → Track in Chong's Tracker.
  • Picture: select the token. Click the × on it to detach.
  • Select a token on the map: its entry jumps to the top.

Entries & tabs

  • Drag a card to reorder. Drop it on a tab to move it.
  • ⋯ on an entry: Max HP, Extra HP, AC, delete.
  • + next to the tabs: new tab. Double-click a tab to rename it.
  • Hidden stats (dimmed): players and the map show only H/B and AC. Toggle in ⋯, or the whole tab with the eye.
  • Door: save the tab to the room, so it's in every scene.
