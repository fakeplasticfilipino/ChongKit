Adding

Type a name and its HP, then press Enter. Goblin x4 15 makes four goblins with 15 HP each. Tack on ac:M, max:70 or extra:5 if you need them, or paste a whole fight from the combat generator: each monster keeps its other lines (damage, save DC…) as its note, and the rest of the fight goes in the tab's note at the top.

Anything called a minion becomes one shared entry: Kobold Minion x10 has 10 HP, one per kobold, and can hold all ten tokens.

Notes

The box at the top of a tab is its note. Each entry has its own note in ⋯. Selecting a token opens its ⋯; a click elsewhere closes it unless it's pinned.

HP

The HP box works like a calculator. -3 is 3 damage, +5 heals 5, and a plain number sets it. Extra HP soaks damage first; healing stops at Max HP.

Tokens

Click an entry's empty circle, then a token on the map. Box-select a group to fill several entries at once, or right-click a token and pick Track. Click a picture to find its token; click the × on it to let go.

Tabs

Drag a card to reorder it, or drop it on a tab to move it there. Double-click a tab to rename it. The door saves a tab to the room, so it follows you into every scene.

Hiding stats

New monsters keep their HP secret: players and the map only see H (healthy) or B (bloodied), plus AC. Flip it per entry in ⋯, or for the whole tab with the eye. /clear empties the tab and its note; /clear Goblin just the goblins.
