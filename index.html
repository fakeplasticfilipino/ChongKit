ChongKit

Tools for our Nimble table.

Combat Generator v2
By the Book. Monster HP, damage and Save DC from the Monster Builder.
Build an encounter for your party, from Easy to Very Deadly, or a legendary solo boss, with generic monsters or named ones from the bestiary. Fresh dice every time, each averaging exactly what the guide says, plus the expected gold reward.
Chong's Tracker Owlbear
Any System. An Owlbear Rodeo extension.
Track token health in tabs, with HP and AC badges on each token. Paste a fight from the Combat Generator to add it in one go.
Chong Die Owlbear
Any System. 3D dice for Owlbear Rodeo.
Type rolls in plain words (4d6 keep 3, 1d10 crit) and save the ones you use as one-click pills. Dice land on the faces the roll decided. Based on Owlbear Rodeo Dice.
Character Sheet v4
Yours to Shape. The Nimble sheet, with boxes you can add and remove.
Hit Points, Wounds, stats with save pips, skills, and tabs of note boxes. A sheet you write on: nothing is worked out for you, and everything saves in your browser.
Campfire Ambience
Around the Fire. A pixel-art night scene for a screen at the table.
The party around a campfire in the forest: flickering light, rising embers, a wizard's glowing staff, a fighter nodding off. Put it on the TV and let it run.
