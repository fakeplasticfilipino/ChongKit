Nimble Combat Generator

Every number comes from the Nimble 5e v2 GM Guide. The damage dice are randomized every time, but each expression averages exactly what the guide says that monster should deal.

Source: Nimble 5e v2 GM Guide v2.0.1 — Monster Builder (p.30), Combat Encounter Guidelines (p.26–27), Running Monsters (p.25), Legendary Monster Builder (p.44), Gold (p.22), Leveling Up (p.75). See docs/rules-reference.md.
